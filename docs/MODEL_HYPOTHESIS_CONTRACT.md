## Planning and execution

A full Meta investigation now proposes structured `hypothesis_tests` before numeric execution. The model chooses predicate kinds and bounded parameters; the backend renders their exact propositions and criteria. It does not infer a test from unchecked prose or accept a model-supplied verdict. Full investigations require three distinct criterion kinds and all six Ghana methods:

- `consistent_high_yield`: at least one complete-coverage genotype ranks within `ceil(panel_size * rank_fraction)` in every observed drought environment; at least three environments are required. The fraction is bounded at 0.05–0.25.
- `leading_rank_sensitivity`: at least one of `leading_count` leading complete-coverage means has an omitted-environment rank range of at least `ceil(panel_size * rank_fraction)`. This tests the existence of a large rank shift, not the absence of shifts or a significance threshold.
- `negative_trait_association`: negative genotype-mean Spearman rho, a conditional genotype-profile resampling interval wholly below zero, and negative directions in every one of at least three observed environments. Mixed directions or a zero-crossing interval are inconclusive. Stay-green means numeric ordinal codes with unverified biological direction.

IDs, parameter compatibility, uniqueness, exact declared propositions and full tool coverage are validated before execution. Additional user hypotheses stay preserved and unassessed; they force partial status rather than disappearing. The older unsupported genotype-specific bootstrap/heterogeneity hypotheses were not retroactively converted into successful criteria. A new plan for the same original objective was explicitly declared and retained.

Tool generation uses per-action schema branches. Nonresampling actions cannot carry an integer resampling parameter. Actual responses are validated before dispatch; rejected decisions, recovery, output exhaustion and budget admission failures remain recorded and prevent a successful Meta acceptance claim.

## Report and scientific boundaries

`scientific_report.py` evaluates the actual declared parameters against saved numeric artifacts, retains contradictions and inconclusive outcomes, and identifies the highest observed drought mean meeting the declared consistency criterion. It reports observed variability, omitted-environment ranks and descriptive retention. The next experiment is a proposed randomized, replicated multi-environment water-regime trial with a check and an appropriate whole-plot/subplot error structure. It does not invent a powered sample size.

The renderer reconstructs the scientific report using the stored predicates and rejects mismatches. The optional `development_audit` bundle field becomes a clearly labelled engineering appendix; it is HTML-escaped and cannot replace numeric evidence. Exact source/schema bytes for the successful execution are archived separately from later readability and deployment-profile changes.

Finite resampling sensitivity remains visible: ASI rho was -0.250356; 1,000 draws gave an interval crossing zero, while 2,000 and 10,000 draws gave intervals narrowly below zero. These are computations on the same data, not independent replications. Field validation, causal tolerance, genotype rank confidence and independent/blind scientific assessment are not established.
